Request deletion of your TekWatt Nexus account

You can request deletion even if you no longer have the TekWatt Nexus app or cannot sign in. Send a request from the email address associated with your account to support@tekwatt.in. If you cannot use that email address, tell us your account email and we will provide another way to verify ownership. Do not send your password, payment card number, or one-time code.

Email an account-deletion request

What happens next

  1. We verify that the request comes from the account owner and check for any active charging or unsettled payments.
  2. We disable the login and remove or de-identify profile and other personal data that is no longer needed.
  3. We confirm completion and explain any records that must be retained.

Charging, invoice, payment, security or dispute records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, tax, fraud-prevention or claims purposes. Deletion of your app account does not cancel an amount already owed or erase records that must legally be kept. Contact support if you have an open charging session or outstanding payment.
