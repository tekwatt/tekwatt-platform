TekWatt Nexus

Privacy policy

Effective 28 September 2026. This policy describes how TEKWATT NEXUS PVT LTD (“TekWatt”, “we”) handles information when you use the TekWatt Nexus driver app and related charging services. Contact us at support@tekwatt.in. Our business location is Velachery, Chennai 600042, Tamil Nadu, India.

Information we process

We process account details such as your name, email address, phone number, workspace, and sign-in credentials; charging-card identifiers and assigned chargers; reservation and charging records, including station, connector, time, energy, status and cost; invoice, wallet and payment status information; support messages; and device, network and security information needed to operate and protect the service. Payment card details are handled by the payment provider rather than stored as card numbers in the driver app.

How we use it

We use this information to provide account access, show stations, authorize and manage charging, handle reservations, calculate charges, issue invoices, process payments, provide support, send service messages and protect the service from misuse. We may use aggregated operational information to improve reliability and reporting.

Service providers

Our service uses infrastructure and delivery providers, including Microsoft Azure for hosting, Google Maps for station maps, Razorpay where in-app payment is enabled, and configured email or SMS providers for messages. Their processing is limited to the information needed for the relevant service and is subject to their own terms and privacy notices.

Location and permissions

The driver app shows registered station coordinates on a map and can open directions in Google Maps. It does not request continuous background location access. Opening directions may share a station destination with your chosen maps app.

Storage and retention

We keep account and operational records while needed to deliver the service. After an account-deletion request, we remove or de-identify the active login and profile data that is no longer needed. We may retain charging, invoice, payment, security and dispute records where required for tax, accounting, fraud prevention or other legal obligations. Retained records are not used to keep the deleted account active.

Your choices

You can update supported profile details in the app. To request access, correction or deletion of your information, contact support@tekwatt.in. For account deletion, use our account-deletion page; it remains available even after uninstalling the app.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above will be updated when we publish a revised version.
